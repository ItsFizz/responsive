OUR STORY

Fresh food starts with knowing where to find it.

Farmers markets play an important role in connecting communities with the growers who bring fresh, seasonal food to local tables. Yet information about market locations, schedules and available produce can often be scattered across different places.

FreshFind was created to bring that information together, making it easier for people to discover local markets and plan their visits around what is available.

01

Discover Local Markets

Why Local Discovery Matters

Finding fresh, locally-sourced food shouldn't require hours of research across multiple websites, social media pages, and community boards. FreshFind consolidates all farmers market information into one easy-to-search platform.

What You Can Find:

  • Market Locations: Precise addresses with integrated maps for easy navigation
  • Search Filters: Find markets by neighborhood, day, or specific produce
  • Market Details: Operating hours, vendor information, and parking availability
  • Visual Previews: Photos to help you know what to expect

The Problem We Solve:

Before FreshFind, discovering local farmers markets meant piecing together information from:

  • Individual market websites (often outdated)
  • Social media posts (scattered and inconsistent)
  • Community bulletin boards (limited reach)
  • Word of mouth (incomplete information)

02

Know What's Fresh

Seasonal Eating Made Simple

Understanding what's in season helps you buy fresher produce, support local farmers, and enjoy better-tasting food at lower prices. FreshFind's produce guide takes the guesswork out of seasonal shopping.

Features of Our Produce Guide:

  • Seasonal Calendar: Know exactly when each fruit or vegetable is at its peak
  • Product Information: Learn about varieties, storage tips, and nutritional benefits
  • Where to Find: See which markets typically carry specific produce
  • Save Favorites: Bookmark produce you love and get notified when it's in season

Benefits of Seasonal Shopping:

  • Better Taste: In-season produce is harvested at peak ripeness
  • Lower Prices: Abundance drives down costs during peak season
  • Nutritional Value: Fresher produce retains more nutrients
  • Environmental Impact: Less transportation means lower carbon footprint
  • Support Local: Buying seasonal supports your local farming community

03

Plan Your Visit

Never Miss a Market Day

Planning ahead ensures you never waste a trip to a closed market. FreshFind provides up-to-date schedules, opening hours, and helpful tips to make your farmers market visit smooth and enjoyable.

Planning Tools We Offer:

  • Real-Time Hours: See current operating hours and days for every market
  • "Open Today" Filter: Quickly find markets operating right now
  • Parking & Access: Information about parking, public transit, and accessibility
  • Reminders: Set alerts for your favorite market days (coming soon)

Make the Most of Your Visit:

  • Arrive Early: Best selection is typically in the first hour
  • Bring Cash: While many vendors accept cards, cash is king at farmers markets
  • Reusable Bags: Bring your own bags to carry purchases
  • Chat with Vendors: They're experts! Ask for recipe ideas and storage tips
  • Check Special Events: Many markets host seasonal events, cooking demos, and live music

What to Expect:

Most farmers markets offer a vibrant community atmosphere with:

  • Fresh produce directly from local farmers
  • Artisan products like honey, jams, and baked goods
  • Prepared foods and food trucks
  • Craft vendors and handmade items
  • Family-friendly activities
