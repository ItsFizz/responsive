01

Overview

FreshFind ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our platform at freshfind.example.

We built FreshFind as a lightweight, client-side platform. That design choice means the vast majority of data — including your account — stays entirely on your own device and is never transmitted to our servers.

02

What We Collect

Here is every type of data that FreshFind touches — and how it is handled:

Data Type Where Stored Sent to Us? Purpose
Account name & email Your browser (localStorage) No Login, personalised greeting
Password (hashed) Your browser (localStorage) No Account authentication
Bookmarks Your browser (localStorage) No Saving favourite markets
Contact form messages Your email app Via email Responding to your enquiry
Market listing submissions Your email app Via email Adding your market to the directory
ℹ️
What we do NOT collect We do not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, browser fingerprinting, or any form of passive user monitoring.
03

How We Use Information

The limited information we receive through contact forms is used only to:

  • Respond to your message or enquiry
  • Add or update market listings you have submitted
  • Address any complaints or support requests

We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing, do not add you to any mailing lists without explicit opt-in, and do not share it with third parties.

04

Local Storage & Cookies

FreshFind uses the browser's localStorage API to store your account credentials and bookmarks. This data:

  • Lives entirely on your device
  • Is never uploaded to any server
  • Persists until you clear your browser data or log out
  • Is only readable by freshfind.example — not by other websites

Session Storage

We use sessionStorage temporarily to remember which page you were trying to access before being redirected to login. This data is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab.

Cookies

FreshFind does not set any cookies of its own. Third-party fonts loaded from Google Fonts may set their own cookies subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

05

FreshFind loads resources from the following third-party providers:

Service Purpose Data Sent
Google Fonts Manrope & Instrument Serif typefaces Your IP address (standard CDN request)

Google Maps Office location map on Contact page Your IP address if map iframe loads

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their individual privacy policies.

06

Your Rights

You have full control over the data FreshFind holds about you:

  • Access & portability — Your account data is in your browser's localStorage; you can inspect it directly using your browser's developer tools.
  • Deletion — Log out from FreshFind to clear your session, or clear your browser's site data to remove all locally stored information.
  • Correction — Create a new account with updated details at any time.
  • Contact data — If you contacted us via email and want that correspondence deleted from our inbox, email us and we will comply promptly.
🗑️
Deleting your data Open browser Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → select "Site settings" or "Cookies and stored data" for freshfind.example. Everything is gone instantly.
07

Children's Privacy

FreshFind is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will assist in removing the relevant data from the device.

08

Security

Since FreshFind stores account data locally on your device, the security of that data depends largely on the security of your own device and browser. We recommend:

  • Using a strong, unique password for your FreshFind account
  • Keeping your browser and operating system up to date
  • Not using FreshFind on shared or public computers while logged in
  • Logging out when you are done if using a shared device

For contact form submissions, emails are transmitted through your standard email client. The security of email in transit is governed by your email provider.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of FreshFind after changes have been posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

We will never make changes that reduce your privacy rights without providing clear notice on the platform.
