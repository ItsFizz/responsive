What is FreshFind and why was it created?

FreshFind is a browser-based platform designed to help residents discover nearby farmers markets. It was created because information about market locations, timings, and available produce is often scattered across flyers, community boards, and social media with no single reliable source.

Is FreshFind free to use?

Yes! FreshFind is completely free for all users. There are no subscription fees, hidden charges, or premium tiers. You can access all features at no cost.

What devices can I use FreshFind on?

FreshFind is fully responsive and works seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. The website adapts to your screen size for optimal viewing.

Do I need to create an account to use FreshFind?

No account is required to browse markets and produce. However, creating an account allows you to save bookmarks, add notes, and personalize your experience.

How often is the information updated?

Market information is regularly updated to ensure accuracy. We work to keep operating hours, locations, and produce availability current.

Can I suggest a market to be added?

Yes! We welcome suggestions for new farmers markets. Please use the contact form or reach out through our support channels with market details.

What is the AI Chatbot feature?

The AI chatbot is a floating widget that helps answer common questions. You can ask about markets, produce, features, and get instant responses based on our knowledge base.

Is my data secure on FreshFind?

Yes, we take data security seriously. Your information is stored securely and we don't share personal data with third parties.

Can I share market information with friends?

Absolutely! Each market page has social sharing options. You can share via social media, email, or copy a direct link.

How do I contact FreshFind support?

You can reach our support team through the Contact page, use the AI chatbot for quick answers, or email us directly. We typically respond within 24 hours.

How do I search for farmers markets near me?

Use the "Find a Market" feature on the home page. You can search by area, neighborhood, or use filters for day of the week and produce type.

What information is shown for each market?

Each market listing includes the complete address, embedded map, operating days and hours, available products, and photos when available.

Can I filter markets by day of the week?

Yes! Use the day filter to find markets open on specific days. This is perfect for planning weekend visits or fitting markets into your weekly schedule.

How accurate are the market operating hours?

We regularly update hours, but we recommend calling ahead during holidays or weather events as markets may adjust schedules.

Do markets require admission fees?

Most farmers markets are free to enter. You only pay for the products you purchase from vendors. Check individual market pages for specific details.

Can I see which markets are open today?

Yes! Use the "Open Today" filter on the Markets page to see which markets are currently operating. Results update based on the current day.

Are parking details provided?

When available, market pages include parking information. The embedded map also helps you identify nearby parking areas.

Can I see photos of the markets?

Many market pages include photos to give you a preview of the atmosphere, vendors, and typical product displays.

What payment methods do markets accept?

Payment methods vary by vendor. Most accept cash, and many also take cards, mobile payments, or EBT/SNAP benefits. Check individual market details.

How do I report incorrect market information?

If you notice outdated or incorrect information, please use the "Report Issue" link on the market page or contact our support team.

What is the Produce Guide?

The Produce Guide is a browsable catalog of fruits, vegetables, herbs, and other products. Each entry includes descriptions, typical seasons, and where to find them.

How do I find seasonal produce?

Browse the Produce Guide and filter by season. Each produce item shows its peak season and availability months.

Can I search for specific produce types?

Yes! Use the search bar in the Produce Guide or filter by category (fruits, vegetables, herbs, dairy, etc.) to find specific items.

Which markets sell a specific produce item?

Each produce page lists markets that typically carry that item. Click on any market to see full details, hours, and location.

Are organic options indicated?

When information is available, we indicate which vendors offer organic or certified options. Check individual market and vendor details.

Can I save my favorite produce items?

Yes! Use the bookmark feature to save produce items you're interested in. Access your saved items anytime from the Bookmarks page.

Do produce pages include recipes?

Currently, produce pages focus on availability and markets. Recipe suggestions may be added in future updates based on user feedback.

How do I know when produce is in season?

Each produce item shows its peak season (spring, summer, fall, winter) and specific months when it's typically available.

Can I filter markets by produce availability?

Yes! On the Markets page, use the produce filter to find markets that typically carry the items you're looking for.

Are specialty items like honey or baked goods listed?

Yes! The Produce Guide includes fruits, vegetables, herbs, dairy, honey, eggs, baked goods, and other artisan products available at markets.
