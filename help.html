FreshFind is a free platform that helps you discover local farmers markets in Karachi. We show you market locations, opening hours, seasonal produce availability, and connect you with fresh local food sources.

The platform works entirely in your browser — no apps to download, no accounts required for browsing. You can search markets by area, day of the week, or type of produce you're looking for.

Features include:

  • Market directory with accurate timings and locations
  • Seasonal produce guides
  • Bookmark system to save favourite markets
  • AI chatbot assistant ("Sprout") for quick questions
  • Contact system for market organisers

Yes! FreshFind is completely free to use. There are no subscription fees, premium tiers, or hidden costs.

We believe access to fresh local food information should be available to everyone.

We work hard to keep market hours accurate, but they can change due to weather, holidays, or other circumstances. Information is updated regularly through:

  • Direct contact with market organisers
  • Community submissions and corrections
  • Regular verification calls

Always double-check with the market directly before making a special trip, especially during Ramadan, Eid, or extreme weather.

Click Login / Signup in the top navigation, then choose Create Account. You'll need:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • A password (minimum 8 characters)

Important: FreshFind accounts are stored locally in your browser, not on external servers. This keeps your data private, but means clearing your browser data will remove your account.

Bookmarks let you save your favourite markets for quick access later. You need a FreshFind account to use bookmarks.

To bookmark a market: Look for the bookmark icon (📖) on market cards and click it. It will turn green when saved.

To view bookmarks: Click the bookmark icon in the top navigation. You'll see all saved markets with their current status (open/closed).

Bookmarks are stored locally on your device and sync across browser tabs.

Seasonal produce refers to fruits and vegetables that are naturally in harvest during specific times of year. Eating seasonally means:

  • Fresher taste — crops are harvested at peak ripeness
  • Better prices — abundant supply keeps costs down
  • Environmental benefits — less transport and storage needed

FreshFind's seasonal guides show what's at its best right now, updated monthly based on local growing cycles.

If Sprout (the chatbot) isn't responding, try these steps:

  • Refresh the page — this reloads the chatbot data
  • Check your connection — the chatbot needs internet to load its knowledge base
  • Clear browser cache — go to Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
  • Try a different browser — some ad blockers interfere with chatbot scripts

If the problem persists, contact our support team — we'll get it sorted quickly.

We'd love to list your market! Contact us with these details:

  • Market name and location (area + landmarks)
  • Days and hours of operation
  • What produce you typically sell
  • Contact information for updates
  • Any seasonal changes or special notes

We'll add your market within 2-3 working days and send you confirmation.

Listing is free — FreshFind doesn't charge market organisers.

Unfortunately, we can't reset passwords because FreshFind accounts are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.

Your options are:

  • Try password variations — common typos or old passwords you might have used
  • Check saved passwords — your browser may have saved it (Settings → Passwords)
  • Create a new account — you'll lose bookmarks but can start fresh

This is a tradeoff of our privacy-first design — your data never leaves your device, so we can't help recover it.

There's no separate app — FreshFind works great in your mobile browser!

The site is fully responsive and optimized for phones. You can even add it to your home screen for app-like access:

  • iPhone: Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
  • Android: Tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen"

This gives you quick access without downloading anything.
